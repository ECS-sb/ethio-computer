## Full description (max 4000)

### English
Ethio Computer is an Ethiopian marketplace for buying and selling social media accounts: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Telegram, Facebook and X.

SAFE FOR BUYERS
• Your money is held by Ethio Computer until you receive the account and confirm it.
• See followers, engagement, audience and screenshots before you make an offer.
• If something goes wrong, open a dispute and our team reviews it.

EASY FOR SELLERS
• List an account in a few minutes and receive offers.
• Verified sellers get a badge, appear first and sell faster.
• Get paid to your wallet after the buyer confirms, then withdraw to your bank account.

TWO WAYS TO COMPLETE A SALE
• Self handover: buyer and seller complete it through the site (10% fee, paid by the seller).
• Agent handover: our agent takes the account, checks it and hands it over (15% fee, paid by the seller).

WALLET IN BIRR
• Add money with Telebirr, Commercial Bank of Ethiopia or Bank of Abyssinia.
• Pay for accounts from your wallet; cancelled orders are refunded to your wallet.

PROTECTED CHAT
• Talk to the other side inside the app. Phone numbers, links and outside contacts are blocked to keep everyone safe from scams.

HELP IN AMHARIC AND ENGLISH
• An AI assistant answers questions about buying, selling, the wallet and fees.
• Our team is on Telegram @ethiocomputerschool and phone 0924888899.

Browsing is free and needs no account. Sign in with Google to buy or sell.

Ethio Computer is run by Ethio Computer School. Before you sell, make sure the platform's rules allow transferring your account.
